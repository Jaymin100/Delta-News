A Message from the Chapter
Zeta Xi Alumni, welcome to first edition of the Knot.

Spring has been an exciting and rewarding semester for our chapter. We welcomed a pledge class of three outstanding new members and have continued strengthening our relationships with the sororities on campus. One highlight of the semester was hosting a successful basement show at our house, made possible through the dedication and leadership of our president, Holden. The energy within the chapter has been incredible. Every passing week lately has been feeling better than the last.

The success we are experiencing this semester builds on an already remarkable year for our chapter. Last semester, we were honored to receive Chapter of the Year, recognizing the collective effort and commitment of our members. In addition, our organization earned Philanthropy of the Year for the fourth consecutive year, a testament to the passion and consistency our members bring to serving the community and supporting meaningful causes. As well as our previous president winning Greek man of the year. These accomplishments reflect the hard work of our executive board and the continued dedication of every member who contributes to making our chapter stronger.

We are incredibly grateful for the support and involvement of our alumni and always enjoy the opportunity to reconnect and build new relationships. Our Spring Formal will take place on April 18th at The Red Carpet, and we would love to see even more of you there. Tickets are $30 and will be linked on this page. Spring is a season of renewal, and we truly feel our chapter and its members continuing to blossom as we look ahead to an even brighter future together.

As always - YITBOS.

Jaymin Swedlund
Sargent at Arms, Fall 2023

Greek Man of the Year
Jaymin "Wendigo" Swedlund
Pledge Class: Fall 2023 · Year: Junior

Jaymin has been a cornerstone of the chapter this last year. He stepped up as president in fall of 2025 and also won Greek man of the year and hosted that year's shave for the cure. He currently holds sergeant and has previously held the position of Secretary and Vice President as well. He was picked out of 50 deltas to be sent to Seattle this winter by nationals for a career accelerator program. Outside of Fraternity life he works at SCSUs system office as a Software engineering intern and will be working at tactile medical this summer as a SWE. He is also an active member in the Judo, CSCI, and competitive programming team. Also hes 6'10.
