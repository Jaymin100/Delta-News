Alumni Network

A Message from the Chapter

Zeta Xi alumni, welcome to the first edition of The Knot.

Spring has been an exciting and rewarding semester for our chapter. We welcomed a pledge class of three outstanding new members and have continued strengthening our relationships with the sororities on campus. One highlight of the semester was hosting a successful basement show at our house, made possible through the dedication and leadership of our president, Holden. The energy within the chapter has been incredible. Every passing week lately has been feeling better than the last.

The success we are experiencing this semester builds on an already remarkable year for our chapter. Last semester, we were honored to receive Chapter of the Year, recognizing the collective effort and commitment of our members. In addition, our organization earned Philanthropy of the Year for the fourth consecutive year, a testament to the passion and consistency our members bring to serving the community. Our previous president also won Greek Man of the Year.

We are incredibly grateful for the support and involvement of our alumni and always enjoy the opportunity to reconnect. Our Spring Formal will take place on April 18th at The Red Carpet, and we would love to see even more of you there. Tickets are $30. Spring is a season of renewal, and we truly feel our chapter continuing to blossom as we look ahead to an even brighter future together.

As always — YITBOS.

Jaymin Swedlund

Sergeant at Arms, Fall 2023

Brothers in Focus

Recognizing brothers who exemplify the ideals of Delta Sigma Phi.

Greek Man of the Year

Jaymin "Wendigo" Swedlund

Pledge Class: Fall 2023 · Year: Junior

Jaymin has been a cornerstone of the chapter over the last year. He stepped up as president in fall 2025, won Greek Man of the Year, and hosted that year's Shave for the Cure. He was selected out of 50 Delta members to be sent to Seattle for a career accelerator program by nationals. Outside of fraternity life, he works at SCSU's System Office as a software engineering intern and will be working at Tactile Medical this summer as a SWE. He is also an active member of the Judo, CSCI, and competitive programming teams. Also, he's 6'10".

"Brotherhood isn't just something you put on a resume, but you totally should."

Best Board Position Development

Sean "Jar Jar" Kelly

Pledge Class: Holy Unc · Year: Second Victory Lap

Sean has been a member of the chapter for many years. His role as pledge educator has been incredible to watch — he has revamped every slideshow using his graphic design background and made a commitment to making sure pledges know their stuff. He also designed our rush banner, makes most of our party flyers, serves as secretary of the Artists Student Union, and has been featured in multiple art shows.

"Time flies like an arrow, fruit flies like a banana."

Past Events

Past Events

Highlights from some of the awesome events our chapter hosted earlier this year.

Deltapalooza

This was the first real basement show we had as a chapter. We sold 67 tickets at $5 and raised over $300 for the chapter. We had 3 bands and some individuals who performed on Friday and Saturday night. It was an amazing night, set up by president Holden "CD" Richardson and Nash Foster Phoenix.

Chili Fest

This was also the first time we did a chili cook-off as a fundraiser! Put on by our actives — it was a super fun event with the brothers and friends of Delta!
