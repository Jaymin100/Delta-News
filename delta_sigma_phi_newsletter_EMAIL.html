A Message from the Chapter
Zeta Xi alumni, welcome to the first edition of The Knot.

Spring has been an exciting and rewarding semester for our chapter. We welcomed a pledge class of three outstanding new members and have continued strengthening our relationships with the sororities on campus. One highlight of the semester was hosting a successful basement show at our house, made possible through the dedication and leadership of our president, Holden. The energy within the chapter has been incredible. Every passing week lately has been feeling better than the last.

The success we are experiencing this semester builds on an already remarkable year for our chapter. Last semester, we were honored to receive Chapter of the Year, recognizing the collective effort and commitment of our members. In addition, our organization earned Philanthropy of the Year for the fourth consecutive year, a testament to the passion and consistency our members bring to serving the community and supporting meaningful causes. Our previous president also won Greek Man of the Year. These accomplishments reflect the hard work of our executive board and the continued dedication of every member who contributes to making our chapter stronger.

We are incredibly grateful for the support and involvement of our alumni and always enjoy the opportunity to reconnect and build new relationships. Our Spring Formal will take place on April 18th at The Red Carpet, and we would love to see even more of you there. Tickets are $30 and will be linked on this page. Spring is a season of renewal, and we truly feel our chapter and its members continuing to blossom as we look ahead to an even brighter future together.

As always - YITBOS.

Jaymin Swedlund
Sergeant at Arms, Fall 2023

Greek Man of the Year
Jaymin "Wendigo" Swedlund
Pledge Class: Fall 2023 · Year: Junior

Jaymin has been a cornerstone of the chapter over the last year. He stepped up as president in fall 2025, won Greek Man of the Year, and hosted that year's Shave for the Cure. He currently serves as sergeant and has previously held the positions of secretary and vice president as well. He was selected out of 50 Delta members to be sent to Seattle this winter by nationals for a career accelerator program. Outside of fraternity life, he works at SCSU's System Office as a software engineering intern and will be working at Tactile Medical this summer as a SWE. He is also an active member of the Judo, CSCI, and competitive programming teams. Also, he's 6'10".

"Brotherhood isn't just something you put on a resume, but you totally should."
Best Board Position Development
Sean "Jar Jar" Kelly
Pledge Class: Holy Unc · Year: Second Victory Lap

Sean has been a member of the chapter for many years. However, this last year he has really stepped up on the E-Board. His role as pledge educator has been incredible to watch. He has revamped every slideshow using his graphic design background and has made a commitment to making sure pledges know their stuff. What he has done for the position is the biggest positive change I have seen in all my time at Delta. Outside the house, the banner was designed by him. Thank you to Steve Poons for production. "Time flies like an arrow, fruit flies like a banana."

Past Events
Past Events
Highlights from some of the awesome events our chapter hosted earlier this year.
Deltapalooza

This was the first real basement show we had as a chapter. It was a great success - we ended up selling 67 tickets at $5 and raised over $300 for the chapter. We had 3 bands and some individuals who performed on Friday and Saturday night. It was such a fun, amazing event. This was set up by our current president Holden "CD" Richardson, as well as Nash Foster and Phoneix.
Chili Fest

This was also the first time we did a chili cook-off as a fundraiser! This was put on by our actives. We didn't end up raising money due to sunk costs, but it was still a super fun event with the brothers and friends of Delta!
Alumni Network
Alumni Shout-Outs
Our alumni are what set us apart from other chapters and fraternities at SCSU. Let's look at some outstanding alumni who supported our chapter this year.
TH
Terry Hollerbach
Fall 1980

Terry was an alumnus who donated $2000 from the Thomas Richard Hollerbach Scholarship. This scholarship is one that Terry made after the passing of his father, after which the scholarship is named. 20% of the money left to him he donated to good organizations. His father believed that you have to be involved in anything you're doing, otherwise someone is going to do it instead. Terry was only an active member for one semester before he graduated, but had so many stories to tell. He was a criminal justice major, but his career was in heavy construction and welding. He was in a frat in high school and loved the idea of continuing this in college. He is the only father-son legacy member. His stepson Mickey Fortune was a member in 1998. During his initiation week Terry was the one to pour a whole 5-gallon bucket on him when he sailed the Nile. Even after 46 years, he still thinks about us and supports our chapter. Thank you so much Uncle Terry!
